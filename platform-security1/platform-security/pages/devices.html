Secure your devices

Device Security

Your phone and computer hold your messages, photos and accounts. A few settings and habits keep them safer.

Phones Computers Malware Protection Software Updates Downloading Safely App Permissions
📱

Phones

  • Set a screen lock: a PIN (6 digits or more), fingerprint or face unlock. Avoid 1234 and your birthday.
  • Turn on automatic updates for the system and your apps
  • Install apps only from the official app store
  • Turn on "Find my device" so you can locate, lock or erase a lost phone
  • Don't leave your phone unlocked and unattended
💻

Computers

  • Lock the screen whenever you step away
  • Use a password or PIN to sign in, and keep it private
  • Keep the system and your browser updated
  • Leave the built-in firewall and security tools turned on
  • On shared computers, log out when you're done and don't save passwords
🛡️

Malware protection

Security software watches for harmful programs and warns you before they cause damage.

  • Use the built-in tool (like Windows Security) or a reputable antivirus, and keep it on
  • Run a full scan now and then, and scan USB drives before opening them
  • Don't ignore security warnings, and don't turn protection off to "make an app work"
🔄

Software updates

Updates often fix security holes that attackers already know about. Putting them off leaves those holes open.

  • Turn on automatic updates wherever you can
  • Restart when your device asks you to
  • Update your apps and browser too, not just the system
  • Replace devices that no longer get updates

🔐 App permissions

Does the app really need that?

Apps ask for access to your camera, microphone, contacts, photos and location. Some need it to work. Others just collect more than they should.

Ask yourself: does this permission match what the app does? A flashlight app doesn't need your contacts.

Good habits

  • Choose "only while using the app" for location when you can
  • Say no to permissions an app doesn't need
  • Check your permission settings now and then and remove what you don't use
  • Uninstall apps you no longer use
