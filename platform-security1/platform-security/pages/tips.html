Small habits. Stronger security. Start with any of these, and go deeper with the full pages.

Account security

  • Use a unique, strong password for every account
  • Turn on two-factor authentication, starting with your email
  • Keep your recovery email and phone number up to date
  • Now and then, check which devices are signed in and sign out of the ones you don't recognize

📱

Device security

  • Set a screen lock on your phone and computer
  • Download apps only from official stores
  • Keep security protection turned on
  • Turn on "Find my device" in case it's lost
Device security →
🌐

Network security

  • Give your home Wi-Fi a strong password
  • Change the router's default login and update its firmware
  • Turn off sharing on networks you don't trust
Network security →
🧭

Safe browsing

  • Check the web address before you sign in, especially after clicking a link
  • A padlock only means the connection is encrypted, not that the site is trustworthy
  • Close pop-ups instead of clicking them, and don't install "required" extensions or plug-ins
  • On shared computers, log out and don't save passwords
🔄

Software updates

  • Turn on automatic updates for your system and apps
  • Restart when your device asks you to, so updates finish installing
  • Update your browser too
Why updates matter →
💾

Backups

  • Keep copies of important files, such as schoolwork and photos
  • Follow the 3-2-1 idea: 3 copies, on 2 kinds of storage, with 1 kept somewhere else (like the cloud)
  • Check once in a while that you can actually open your backup
Why backups save you →
☕

Public Wi-Fi

  • Avoid banking and other sensitive activity when you can
  • Stick to HTTPS websites and keep your device updated
  • Turn off automatic connection to unknown networks
Public Wi-Fi checklist →
