📋 Printable checklist

Your security habits, on paper

Print this, tick the boxes and stick it somewhere you'll see it.

  • I use a unique password for every account
  • I use two-factor authentication on my important accounts
  • I keep my software and apps updated
  • I avoid suspicious links and unexpected attachments
  • I review my privacy settings
  • I back up my important files
  • I secure my Wi-Fi

🚨 If something happens

Something Went Wrong? Start Here.

Pick what happened for simple steps. Acting quickly helps, and it's not your fault.

🎣 I clicked a phishing link
  1. Close the page and don't enter anything else.
  2. If you typed a password, change it right away (from the official app or website), and anywhere you reused it.
  3. Turn on two-factor authentication and check for activity you don't recognize.
  4. Run a security scan if you downloaded anything.
  5. Report the message to the platform, your school or your IT team.
🔑 My password was compromised
  1. Change that password now, and make it long and unique.
  2. Change it on any other account where you used the same one.
  3. Turn on two-factor authentication.
  4. Sign out of all other devices if the account lets you.
  5. Consider a password manager so every account gets its own password.
🦠 I downloaded a suspicious file
  1. Don't open it. If you already did, disconnect from the internet.
  2. Run a full scan with your security software.
  3. Delete the file and uninstall anything you don't recognize.
  4. From a clean device, change the passwords for your important accounts.
  5. If things still act strangely, ask your school or IT team for help.
📱 My device was lost
  1. Try to locate, lock or erase it with "Find my device".
  2. Change the passwords for your email and most important accounts.
  3. Contact your mobile provider about blocking your SIM.
  4. If you use banking or e-wallet apps, contact the provider.
  5. If it was stolen, report it to the police.
👤 Someone accessed my account
  1. Try to sign in and change your password. If you're locked out, use the platform's account recovery.
  2. Sign out of all devices and turn on two-factor authentication.
  3. Check your recovery email and phone number haven't been changed.
  4. Warn your contacts not to trust strange messages from you.
  5. Report the problem to the platform's support.
