👤 Personal information

What should you be careful about?

Small details add up. Together, they can be enough to pretend to be you or to find you.

Never share
  • Passwords and verification codes
  • Government ID numbers
  • Bank, card and e-wallet PINs
  • Account login details
Share very carefully
  • Home address and live location
  • Phone number
  • School name, schedule and student number
  • Photos of IDs, documents and tickets
Usually fine
  • Your interests and hobbies
  • Public achievements you're proud of
  • Opinions you're comfortable owning
  • Photos without private details in the background

💬 Social media security

Think before you share

Ask yourself "Should I post this?" for each of these six posts.

Social media habits

  • Set your account to the privacy level you actually want, and check who can see your posts
  • Turn on tag review so you approve posts you're tagged in
  • Don't share your live location, and post trip photos after you're back
  • Be careful with friend requests from people you don't know
  • Look through old posts and remove what you wouldn't share today

🗂️ Data protection

Keep control of your data

Every app and website you sign up for collects some of your information. You can't avoid that completely, but you can decide how much to give.

Companies can be hacked, so the less you hand over, the less there is to leak.

What you can do

  • Give only what's needed when you sign up, and skip optional questions
  • Read what permissions an app asks for before you accept
  • Delete accounts and apps you no longer use
  • Review the privacy settings of your main accounts regularly
  • Keep private documents and photos in a protected place, not in public folders or shared chats
