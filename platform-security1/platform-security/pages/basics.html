Security Basics

A simple, jargon-free introduction to staying safe online. No experience needed.

The idea

What is platform security?

Every app, website and device you use is a platform: social media, email, your school portal, your phone. Platform security means keeping the accounts, information and devices on those platforms safe from people who shouldn't have access to them.

Think of it like locking your house. You don't expect a break-in every day, but a good lock, closed windows and a spare key kept somewhere safe make one much less likely.

In one sentence: security is a set of small habits that make it harder for the wrong people to get into your accounts, devices and information.

Why it matters

What could you lose?

You don't need to be famous or rich to be a target. These are the everyday things worth protecting.

💬

Your social media

If someone takes over your account, they can post as you and message your friends and family.

✉️

Your email

Email can reset the passwords of your other accounts, so it is the key to everything else.

🎓

Your school account

Your classwork, grades and personal details live here.

📱

Your phone

It holds your messages, contacts, apps and often your banking and e-wallet accounts.

🖼️

Your photos

Private photos and documents can be misused if they leak.

🪪

Your personal information

Your name, birthday, address and ID numbers can be used to pretend to be you.

The goals

Three goals of security

Almost every security tip supports one of these three ideas.

🔒

Keep it private

Only the people you allow can see your information. Strong passwords and privacy settings help here.

✅

Keep it trustworthy

Your information can't be changed or faked without you knowing. Updates and careful downloads help here.

🔄

Keep it available

You can reach your accounts and files when you need them. Backups help here.
